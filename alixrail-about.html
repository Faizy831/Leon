RAILWAY MAINTENANCE, REINVENTED

Our Story

ALIXRAIL was born from a simple observation: existing railway measurement tools are no longer suited to the challenges of today's maintenance work. Designed by rail people, for rail people, our instruments combine lightness and agility, for data that's accurate and easy to use.

Built for the field agent

Every ALIXRAIL tool assembles without any tools, operates solo, and connects via Bluetooth to a smartphone or tablet — for real-time measurements and data export from the first minute on site.

Field-ready

Light and rugged enough to work anywhere on the track, in any railway environment.

Self-sufficient

Smartphone-driven, so one operator measures, records, and shares data without extra hands.

Precise

Real-time data, captured and validated on the spot — trusted the moment it's recorded.

Track Position & Clearance Gauge Measurement Trolley
One operator, one pass. Simultaneously measures track position, cant, and left/right clearance gauges across all railway environments. 8 kg · folds to 90 cm · integrated dead-man safety device · real-time app view · direct Excel export

Track Twist & Geometry Measurement Trolley
High-precision geometry, on the spot. Measure, record, and validate track geometry in real time from a smartphone — no heavy equipment, no deferred post-processing. Under 5 kg · cant, twist & odometry on a reference base · TGR-compliant · EN 13848 certified
TOM
Tracking Made Simple
Rail Chainage & Field Event Tracking Tool
The field team's benchmark. Measure rail chainage with precision and log events in seconds via intuitive dropdown menus — all from a single smartphone. Auto-timestamped & geotagged · zero manual transcription · direct export to study systems
KAT
Precision Captured Securely
Catenary Contact Wire Height & Stagger Measurement
Safety without disruption. Acquire catenary geometry data safely outside the danger zone — no catenary consignment, no traffic immobilization. Motorized rangefinder · smartphone-operated · real-time video targeting · simultaneous height & stagger
HALO
Self-reliant. Contact-free.
Autonomous Optical Odometry Tool
Distance you can trust, without touching the rail. Measures odometry — distance and position along the track — using optical sensing instead of a contact wheel, removing wear, slippage, and calibration drift. Fully autonomous · data sent directly to your phone in real time · zero contact with the rail

All ALIXRAIL products export data in open formats (HTML, ASCII CSV) — fully compatible with your existing infrastructure management systems, with data direct on your phone from the first measurement.
